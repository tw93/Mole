FAQ

Pricing questions.

Why keep a free plan?

Roomy's core value is trust. A free plan lets users inspect the tool, run dry previews, and validate the cleanup model before paying for convenience or support.

What does paid support unlock?

Paid support does not unlock hidden cleanup features. It buys priority triage, rollout guidance, and release support around the same GPL CLI.

Is $19/year too low?

It is intentionally below broad cleaner suites because the CLI stays free and the paid plan funds maintenance rather than exclusive product capability.
