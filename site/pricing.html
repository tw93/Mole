Positioning

Undercuts CleanMyMac because the product is narrower.

Roomy does not need to price like a broad consumer suite with malware scanning, cloud cleanup, and a polished native app. The paid plan is for repeatable cleanup convenience and support around a transparent local tool.

FAQ

Pricing questions.

Why keep a free plan?

Roomy's core value is trust. A free plan lets users inspect the tool, run dry previews, and validate the cleanup model before paying for convenience or support.

What should Pro unlock?

The clean split is convenience rather than core safety: profile presets, scheduled recipes, richer exports, priority support, and early access.

Is $19/year too low?

It is intentionally below broad cleaner suites. Roomy is a focused local toolkit, so launch pricing should feel lightweight while the product proves retention.
