Trusted Cleanup

Preview every cleanup before Roomy moves a file.

Roomy is for Mac users, developers, and small teams that need storage back without trusting a black box. It combines cleanup, uninstall, project artifact, installer, duplicate scan, status, and report workflows in one auditable CLI.

How It Fits

A focused alternative to opaque cleaner suites.

Roomy is not trying to be a generic “make my Mac faster” button. It is a transparent local cleanup tool for people who want to inspect, automate, and recover from maintenance work.

Common option Typical promise Where Roomy is different
One-click cleaner suites Broad cleanup and performance guidance wrapped in a polished consumer app. Roomy is narrower and easier to inspect: dry-run previews, explicit risk caps, local execution, and cleanup journals.
Visual disk mappers Beautiful storage inspection that helps users decide what to remove manually. Roomy pairs inspection with guarded cleanup actions for developer caches, app leftovers, installers, and reports.
Generic cleanup utilities Simple clutter removal, duplicate cleanup, app management, and paid upgrade paths. Roomy earns technical credibility with documented safety gates, command output, API contracts, and repeatable workflows.
Dedicated app uninstallers Application removal plus related files, extensions, startup items, and leftover scans. Roomy includes uninstall safety alongside cleanup, project artifact removal, installer cleanup, restore previews, and reporting.
