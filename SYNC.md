## 2026-10-25 Existing uninstall, cleanup, optimization and status follow-up

Frozen source `973c6e1d` on `windows`, matching `origin/windows` after fetch; Mole CLI (macOS) was `ba96efe8` on `main`. Existing owner rows below retain their IDs. Implementation commits are `c1ee99eb` (uninstall content), `b54d26ec` (cleanup/optimization) and `792e08dd` (battery completeness). Source changes in this batch are not a new release or third-party app uninstall acceptance.

- Error reports honor their seven-day retention boundary, including the newest file in a WER report; the final guard rejects a recently changed report. Prefetch's unused default step is removed. - Sublime Text covers both Local and existing Roaming cache leaves through current owner and lock checks. Adobe Team Projects Cache is retained, as recorded under CLI-SYNC-09.
- Optimize checks the actual target disk before disk writes, does not label unknown health critical, and skips disk optimization on battery power or unknown power evidence. A complete no-battery inventory permits desktop maintenance. Startup registrations remain visible without treating their count as a fault. The GUI's scheduled 30-day cadence is an Intentional difference from this explicit CLI command; the existing explicit network-maintenance surface remains unchanged.
- Status combines all present system-battery capacities. Missing capacity for any present pack withholds combined health instead of reusing the first pack; empty bays are ignored, cycles use the oldest pack, and the system-wide charge percentage retains its existing provider. Multi-pack fixtures do not replace physical multi-battery acceptance.

Uninstall's registered vendor, MSIX, timeout, surviving-registration and shared-folder protections were reviewed and regression-tested. Its existing install-folder cleanup now also retains generic game/emulator content at the root or immediate-child level, using `Test-MoleInstallContentFree` through discovery and the final ownership guard. Unknown, linked or over-budget scans retain the folder; `tests/Uninstall.Tests.ps1` covers late-created saves and ordinary/individual-Steam-game controls. No broad personal AppData/registry review or private per-app path catalog was added. Analyze remains a directory explorer; GUI growth history, MFT scans and treemaps are Intentional differences, since the bounded navigation cache is not a stable historical attribution store. No command, flag, setting or background collector is added.

[The development record](docs/development-record.md) owns the complete receipt and the unchanged advisory-lint finding, rather than an all-linters-clean claim. These are source and fixture results, not a new package, a real third-party uninstall, or a completed remote-delivery claim.
