# mo slim

`mo slim` removes Intel slices (`x86_64`, `x86_64h`, `i386`) from universal apps on Apple Silicon. It is the one named exception to "do not rewrite third-party app bundle contents" in `AGENTS.md` (maintainer decision 2026-10-19, after the feature was judged too heavy for Mole Mac: modest savings, restored by every app update, and a deletion surface the app could not explain in its UI). Keep it narrow; a wider scope needs a new maintainer decision.

## Scope

- Only apps in `/Applications` and `~/Applications` or one plain folder below them such as `Utilities` (#1811, maintainer 2026-10-23), never a folder that is itself a bundle or a symlink and never deeper; apps owned by the invoking user with every file inside owned by them, in a writable folder, signed with a Developer ID (`Authority=Developer ID Application`, a 10-character Team ID). App Store (`_MASReceipt`), `com.apple.*`, ad-hoc and unsigned apps, originals Gatekeeper already rejects, `should_protect_from_uninstall` and official-uninstaller vendors, apps set to "Open using Rosetta" (`LSArchitecturesForX86_64` in the LaunchServices plist) and symlinks are out.
- Gatekeeper is asked about every candidate original during the scan, each in the background as soon as its plan is ready, so the checks overlap the rest of the scan (0.2 to 1.4 s each in sequence on the maintainer's Mac, about 3.5 s for an Electron app on another), with a 20 s budget. Only an explicit rejection (`spctl` exit 3) leaves the app out, since its copy can never pass; a timeout or any other error is unknown and keeps the app listed for the copy check to decide. On 2026-10-24 unnotarized builds of Mole, Yado and Hina were offered, copied and then refused one by one. Locked by `slim leaves out apps Gatekeeper already rejects but keeps unknown answers` in `tests/slim.bats`.
- No sudo, no helper, no new privilege. Root-owned apps stay out rather than prompting.
- Reuse the existing Mole whitelist for Slim exclusions, including protected children of an app. Load it before scanning, reload before each app attempt and again immediately before the swap. Unreadable configuration retains the original. Do not add a separate exclusion flag or treat signature verification as proof of compatibility with an app's own integrity checks. Locked by the whitelist cases in `tests/slim.bats`.
- Running apps are listed (Stash and other menu bar apps are always running and would otherwise vanish without a reason) and refused when slimming starts and again right before the swap. The confirmation tags them `[Running]` so they can be quit before Enter instead of being discovered after the run.
- Output follows `mo uninstall`: `◎ name , size` rows, a numbered `✓`/`◎` line per app when several are selected, and a summary that lists slimmed apps three per line and groups unchanged apps by reason (six running apps are one line plus one next action). Locked by `slim summary lists slimmed apps and groups kept apps by reason` in `tests/slim.bats`.
- Without a terminal, or with `--dry-run`, it only lists. Nothing is preselected; no `--yes`, no flags beyond `--dry-run` and `--debug`. A failed confirmation read cancels rather than becoming Enter, locked by `slim confirmation requires a successful Enter read` in `tests/slim.bats`.
- Interactive and read-only lists share a compact name column bounded by terminal width, reserving space for the selection marker and reclaimable size. Pass full, terminal-safe names separately to the shared selector so truncation never changes search or name order. Locked by the `slim rows fit`, `slim searches the full name` and `slim name sorting` cases in `tests/slim.bats`.

## What may be thinned

A Mach-O is thinned only when it is its bundle's main executable (`Contents/MacOS/<CFBundleExecutable>`, or `Versions/<v>/<CFBundleExecutable>` of a framework) or a `files2` entry of the nearest `_CodeSignature/CodeResources` sealed by `cdhash`. Everything sealed by plain `hash2` is left alone, and the file scan never enters `Resources` or `_CodeSignature`. Either way, a file that any enclosing seal up to the app lists by plain hash stays too: ScreenFlow signs its QuickLook plugin, yet ScreenFlow's own seal lists `ScreenFlow.qlgenerator/Contents/MacOS/ScreenFlow` by `hash2`, so thinning the plugin's main executable failed the copy's deep strict verify; Stash seals `Stash Dashboard.app` and its system extension executable the same way. `slim_sealed_keys` therefore prints every `files2` key tagged `c` (cdhash) or `h` (anything else). Locked by `slim never thins an executable an enclosing seal lists as a plain file`. Signatures are per slice, so the arm64 slice keeps its cdhash and nothing is ever re-signed.

Counterexample that set this rule: thinning every universal file outside `Resources` broke Claude's (Electron) seal, because `Electron Framework.framework/Versions/A/Libraries/libffmpeg.dylib` and `libvk_swiftshader.dylib` are sealed by hash. App Store appex and Squirrel's ShipIt behave the same.

## Replace only a verified copy

Clone the bundle with `ditto --clone` into this run's temp folder, thin the planned files, then require on the copy: every planned file is now a thin Apple Silicon Mach-O (deep strict verify alone passed a copy whose arm64 slice had been removed), `codesign --verify --deep --strict`, the same Team ID, and `spctl --assess --type execute`. The original must pass the same deep strict verify first, since a signature that is already broken proves nothing about the copy. The one failure that check forgives is `resource fork, Finder information, or similar detritus not allowed`: `com.apple.FinderInfo` and `com.apple.ResourceFork` xattrs on bundle folders sit outside the seal, and on 2026-10-21 they made Google Chrome and ProtonVPN, both notarized and accepted by Gatekeeper, report "code signature already invalid". Those two xattrs are then stripped on the copy only, with `/usr/bin/xattr -rsd` so a symlink inside the copy never carries the strip to a file outside it, and the copy must pass deep strict verify before any file is thinned, standing in for the original; the original keeps its xattrs. Any failure discards the copy and keeps the app untouched. Only then is the original moved to the Trash through `mole_delete` (identity-pinned) and the copy renamed into place; a failed rename reports that the original is in the Trash. INT and TERM are held from the Trash move until the rename, because the cleanup trap deletes the work folder and the copy with it. Each slimmed app logs one `REBUILT` row.

Slimming needs no developer tools. `lipo` ships only with the Command Line Tools (over 1 GB, more than most Macs reclaim here) and `/usr/bin/lipo` opens their installer when they are missing, so `slim_fat_slices` reads `FAT_MAGIC` and `FAT_MAGIC_64` headers with `od`, and the slimmed file is the single Apple Silicon slice cut out with `tail`/`head`: on 2026-10-19 all 816 eligible universal files in the maintainer's `/Applications` came out byte-identical to `lipo -thin`, with matching Intel byte counts. Only an `arm64` slice is kept: a file with any `arm64e` slice, or any slice that is neither Apple Silicon nor Intel, is skipped rather than given a rebuilt fat header, since macOS refuses third-party arm64e code unless its ABI is enabled and such an app may be running its Intel slice. The slice is cut with `tail | head`, where `tail` dies of SIGPIPE whenever the slice is not the last one, so that status is ignored and the size and Mach-O checks decide. `ditto --arch arm64` was tried as the built-in alternative and copies files unchanged on macOS 27. The host check reads `hw.optional.arm64`, because `uname -m` says `x86_64` inside a Rosetta shell, and calls `/usr/sbin/sysctl` by absolute path: a shell PATH without `/usr/sbin` failed the lookup and refused every Apple Silicon Mac as Intel (#1804).

Slice output is allocated exclusively with `mktemp` beside the copied Mach-O. A bundle may already contain any fixed suffix, including a symlink, so never redirect into a predictable `$file.mole-slim` path. Failed writes or replacements remove only that newly allocated output. Locked by `slim slice output never follows a pre-existing suffix symlink` in `tests/slim.bats`.

## Rejected

- Ad-hoc re-signing (MacSai): drops the Team ID, so keychain items, TCC grants and launch checks break.
- Thinning everything outside `Resources`, or inside it (Pearcleaner strips Resources and keeps no backup; ScreenFlow's `Resources/screenflowrecorder` breaks the seal).
- A Mole Mac review row or result page, root-owned apps through sudo, App Store apps (restored on the next update and protected by the receipt).

Measured on the maintainer's Mac on 2026-10-19: 14 eligible apps, 1.57 GB of Intel code; a Claude copy went from 916 MB to 694 MB and NetNewsWire from 22 MB to 13 MB, both passing deep strict verify and Gatekeeper, and the slimmed NetNewsWire launched. Sparkle delta updates fail their before-hash check on a slimmed app and fall back to a full download, which brings the Intel code back.

Locked by `tests/slim.bats`: the one-level folder scan, the cdhash parser, the plan excluding a hash-sealed tool, the verified swap, Gatekeeper failure keeping the original, both running-app refusals, the fat and fat64 header parser against `lipo`, a run with `lipo` and `xcode-select` made to fail, the Intel-host refusal, the host check ignoring a `sysctl` earlier in PATH, Finder info stripped on the copy only and never through a symlink out of it, a broken seal behind Finder info still kept, a slice cut whose `tail` dies of SIGPIPE, an arm64e slice left alone, an interrupt during the swap still leaving the slimmed app in place, each observed red against the broken criterion.
